01 / PROFILE

Systems level
thinking.

Engineering for me is not only about writing, debugging and learning new implementations of code. It is about understanding how the pieces interact,how it can be scaled in future, how it can be debugged block by block and adapted to future updates easily defining the contract between them and making the result verifiable.

Systems-oriented engineer with ~2.5 years in automotive HMI and UX Design at Daimler Truck Innovation Centre, now deepening into robotics and automated driving with an M.Sc. at TU Dortmund.

I enjoy the unglamorous core of engineering: deriving requirements, evaluating trade-offs and working through hard problems with a team. Automating small processes and most importantly reducing any kind of possible errors throughout the implementation

My instinct is to take the risk of something tangled — a warning database at risk of loss, overflow bugs hiding in 36 languages, or a full autonomy stack — and reverse-engineer it into structure someone else can build on.

Currently co-leading the software-stack workpackage on a teleoperated autonomous driving platform, authoring the interface architecture that acts as the team's system-level contract.
