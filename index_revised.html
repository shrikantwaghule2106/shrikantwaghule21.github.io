Profile

who / how / why

Systems-oriented engineer with ~2.5 years in automotive HMI and ADAS at Daimler Truck Innovation Centre, now deepening into robotics and automated driving with an M.Sc. at TU Dortmund.

I enjoy the unglamorous core of engineering: deriving requirements, evaluating trade-offs, and working through hard problems with a team. My instinct is to take something tangled — a warning database at risk of loss, overflow bugs hiding in 36 languages, a full autonomy stack — and reverse-engineer it into structure someone else can build on.

Currently co-leading the software-stack workpackage on a teleoperated autonomous driving platform, authoring the interface architecture that acts as the team's system-level contract.

Working style

  • Requirements-first, trade-off driven
  • Reverse-engineers to understand
  • Fast to pick up new stacks & tools
  • Documents the contract, not just the code
